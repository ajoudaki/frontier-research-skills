One more thing that matters a lot: reading. Model output is full of compressed notation. They'll invent dozens of new symbols to say in a line what would take pages, and no human can hold that in their head. So I have skills whose only job is to make the model explain things to me. explain-with-canonical-notation makes it use familiar notation and define every symbol before using it, instead of a private alphabet of aliases. teach-technical-math teaches me a paper, or a report the agents wrote, one block at a time. And when I'm confused, it rebuilds the whole explanation instead of patching one sentence. Basically, you write skills that teach the model how to talk to you.
