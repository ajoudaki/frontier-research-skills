Research with AI agents

Two smaller things that make a big difference. First, reading. Model output is full of compressed notation: they'll invent dozens of new symbols to say something that would take pages, and that's very hard for a human to follow. So I have skills whose job is to teach me: familiar notation, every symbol defined, one block at a time. Second, routing. A skill is just a markdown file, and it can be hierarchical. Say I want a skill for Tensor Programs: that's six papers, around a hundred pages each. I don't want all of that loaded into every prompt. So the skill has a table that says: if you need this, load that file. AGENTS.md is always read, so keep it small. Everything else loads only when it's needed.
