So the strategy rests on those foundations, and it's implemented as five skills. Here they are at a glance, from an open-ended research question to a paper. organize-research-project is the main one: it organizes a whole project, one big open question, into many separate thrusts, and builds the established core. Inside each thrust, formulate-problem pins down its question, solve-problem solves it, with every step checked, and teach-material makes sure you understand the result. Finally, review-paper audits the part of the core that becomes a paper, and its findings can start the next round. Let's start with the main one.
