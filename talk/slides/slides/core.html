Research with AI agents

At the project level, the structure is this. Research means pushing many thrusts in different directions, and most of them fail or turn out useless. So each one lives in its own study, an isolated folder. My AGENTS.md, which every agent reads, says that agents work only inside their own study and can't look at the others. That matters, because once an agent picks up a wrong approach, it tends to follow it forever. Isolation stops mistakes from spreading, and it keeps exploration creative. But pure isolation means reinventing the wheel. So there's also an established core of theory and code. A result gets in only through promotion gates: an independent audit, plus integration, meaning the same notation, tests, and clean interfaces. And I approve every promotion myself. That core is the high-signal part. Future studies can use its theorems and its code, and when I read something there, it's worth reading properly.
