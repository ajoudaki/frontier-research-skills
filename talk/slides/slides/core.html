True? Checked? Adopted? Kept separate.

That isolation rule scales up to the whole project, which is what organize-research-project is really about. Research means pushing many thrusts in different directions, and most of them fail. So each one lives in its own study, an isolated folder, and my AGENTS.md tells every agent to stay inside its own study. Once an agent picks up a wrong approach, it tends to follow it forever; isolation stops that from spreading, and keeps exploration creative. But pure isolation means reinventing the wheel. So there's also an established core of theory and code. A result gets in only through promotion gates: an independent audit, plus integration, meaning shared notation, tests, and clean interfaces. And I approve every promotion myself. That core is where the signal is high. Future studies can build on its theorems and its code, and when I read something there, it's worth reading properly. The skill keeps three questions separate: is a result true, has it been checked, and has it been adopted. And it comes with templates for AGENTS.md and for each study.
