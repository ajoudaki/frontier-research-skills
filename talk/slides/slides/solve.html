proved: this thrust is done

Here's the thrust again: from what we know to the frozen question. The arrow between them is the proof, and it's still dashed. Solving is the second hard push, and it starts from the frozen contract. With your go-ahead, the proof search runs as parallel agents, one per route, and each route uses a genuinely different mechanism: duality, induction, an extremal argument. They all get the same contract, and none is told which strategy to favor, so they stay independent. After every round, a registry keeps score. Two routes that turn out to use the same idea are merged. A route that needs a lemma as hard as the theorem itself is marked blocked, but its valid lemmas are kept. Mature routes can borrow from each other: here duality supplies the missing lemma, and induction becomes a candidate proof. But a candidate isn't a proof yet. Separate checks rebuild every implication from the contract, check each cited theorem and its hypotheses, and hunt for counterexamples at the boundary and in degenerate cases. Only what survives gets written up by solve-problem: the plan first, every nontrivial step derived, no 'clearly' in place of a reason, and an answer to exactly the question in the contract. And now the arrow is solid: the thrust is done, most likely correctly.
