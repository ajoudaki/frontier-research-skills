Remember the gem in the haystack? Here's what a real project looks like over time. From the research question, studies branch out. Some pass the gate, and the core forms around them, like a lake. Studies keep branching, level by level. By default they don't see each other, but with your approval one can build on another, even on work that hasn't been promoted yet. So the graph gets large and tangled, while the core grows slowly, and always in one piece. In the end, the core is about a fifth of everything: an ocean of knowledge that's true and relevant, with a long shoreline to the unknown. But a paper isn't the whole core. You carve out a coherent part, grown from the original question, and it has to pass new gates: novelty and significance. That's the audit. So out of 218 studies, 10 become the paper. That's the gem in the haystack. So let me wrap up.
