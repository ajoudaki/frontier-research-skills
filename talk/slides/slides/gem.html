Remember the gem in the haystack? Here's what a real project looks like over time. From the research question, studies branch out. Some pass the gate, and the core forms around them, like a lake. New studies branch off what passed, level by level, and the lake grows with them: an ocean of knowledge you can trust. But look at its shore. The larger the lake, the longer its shoreline with the unknown, and that's where the next studies go. And the haystack around it, you don't have to read. You read the core, and everything in it was formulated, solved, understood and approved. Then the core goes through the audit and becomes a paper. That's the gem. So let me wrap up.
