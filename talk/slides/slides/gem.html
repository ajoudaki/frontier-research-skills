Let's go back to where we started: the gem in the haystack. Here's what a real project looks like over time. From the research question, studies branch out. Some pass the gate and join the core; most don't. New studies branch off what passed, level by level. And the core grows with them, into this organic shape. The project keeps growing, and the more it grows, the smaller the core is in comparison. That's the point. You don't have to read the haystack. You read the core, and everything in it was formulated, solved, understood and approved. Then the core goes through the audit and becomes a paper. That's the gem. So let me wrap up.
