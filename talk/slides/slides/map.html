04 · Two scales

One question, or a whole project.

One question

a single, targeted point

Formulate

investigate-conjectures

A vague question → a sharp conjecture

Solve

solve-math-rigorously

A formal question → a rigorous solution

↑ every thrust in a project runs this pair

A whole project

one big, open question

Develop

organize-research-project

Many thrusts → one trusted core

Audit

review-ai-paper

A finished paper → a cold, rigorous audit

↺ a review's findings can start the next round

They come at two scales. Sometimes you have a targeted, well-scoped question. For that there are two skills: investigate-conjectures sharpens the question, and solve-math-rigorously solves it. But most open research isn't a single question. It's one big open question you push in many directions. For that, organize-research-project develops the project, and review-ai-paper audits the finished paper.
