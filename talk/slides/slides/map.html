04 · Two scales

One question, or a whole project.

One question

a single, targeted point

Formulate

formulate-problem

Vague → precise question

Solve

solve-problem

Question → checked proof

Learn

teach-material

Result → understood

↑ every thrust in a project runs these three

A whole project

one big, open question

Develop

develop-project

Many thrusts → one core

Audit

review-paper

Paper → rigorous audit

↺ a review's findings can start the next round

They work at two scales. Sometimes you have a targeted, well-scoped question. For that there are three skills: formulate-problem sharpens the question, solve-problem solves it, and teach-material helps you understand the result. But most open-ended research isn't a single question. It's one big open question you push in many directions. For that, develop-project develops the project, and review-paper audits the finished paper. Let's start with the single question.
