Raw output

Once you have that, you hit the real bottleneck: you. These models produce code and theory much faster than you can read or check it. Some of it is wrong. Some of it isn't relevant. Some of it quietly answers a different question. So you end up with a huge pile of results: a few gems hidden in a haystack, and no easy way to find them. That can be overwhelming enough that you just give up. So the whole game becomes signal-to-noise. You build workflows that filter the pile, and you only read what has climbed high enough to be worth your time.
