Plain markdown, each skill standalone. The bigger ones keep a short SKILL.md that routes to reference files, loaded only when needed.

Numbers on the right: the slides where each skill came up

Everything I've talked about is in the repo, as seven skills. They're plain markdown files, written for Codex, and each one stands on its own, so you can take just the one you need. The biggest is investigate-conjectures. It's the research controller, with reference files for the research contract, the evidence ledger, the adversarial audit, and pre-registered experiments. organize-research-project sets up the studies, the promotion gates, and the AGENTS.md template. review-ai-paper and solve-math-rigorously are the auditors for papers and for proofs. explain-with-canonical-notation and teach-technical-math make the output readable for me. And handoff-codex-project-tasks moves agent sessions between machines. The numbers on the right show where each one came up in this talk.
