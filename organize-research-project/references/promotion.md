# Incorporate research into maintained theory and code

Promotion changes what future studies may rely on as an accepted project
dependency. Preserve the distinction between a useful exploratory finding and
a maintained result with durable supporting material.

## Choose the review policy before applying it

Follow the adopted project's reviewer roles, depth, and acceptance authority.
If none exists, resolve those choices before declaring a promotion accepted.
An ordinary policy can use an independent scientific reviewer and an explicit
integration check. For foundational or widely reused additions, consider two
fresh independent reviews and a separate integration reviewer. These are
profiles to choose, not universal counts for every project or typo correction.

Missing required reviewers, reproduction inputs, or checks remain visible gaps.
Do not relabel a self-check as independent review or infer authorization from
this skill. Reuse explicit approval already given for an unchanged package.
If approval is still required, prepare the concrete reviewable candidate first.

## Prepare and assess the candidate

1. **Assess relevance and placement.** Identify the useful addition, duplication,
   assumptions, maintenance cost, and smallest suitable destination. Select a
   coherent place in the maintained theory and APIs. A correct result may remain
   in its study if it adds little to that maintained corpus.
2. **Prepare complete material.** Include the precise statement, assumptions,
   full proof or method, necessary dependency arguments, reusable source,
   meaningful verification, and reproduction recipes for empirical claims.
   Use the project's notation and documentation conventions.
3. **Freeze the candidate and dependencies.** Identify actual contents, versions,
   authors, included source inputs, and the neutral review assignment. A commit
   plus hashes for relevant dirty files can identify a snapshot; a mutable path
   alone cannot. Record the old verdict with its old version after corrections.
4. **Obtain the required scrutiny.** Reviewers inspect the full assigned argument,
   code, dependencies, and evidence, including edge cases and the experimental
   producer. Supply missing inputs before treating a review as complete. Keep
   independent reviewers separate from candidate production and prior verdicts
   where the chosen policy requires it.
5. **Check the assembled addition.** Verify source integration, interfaces,
   notation, references, required builds, and reproduction from maintained
   inputs. The accepted artifact must work without study-only scripts, private
   conversation context, or unexplained historical arrays. Stored data may be
   necessary, but their identity and retrieval must be maintained too.
6. **Decide and integrate.** The designated authority accepts the specific checked
   package under the local authorization policy. Recheck current dependencies
   and concurrent changes, apply that package, and verify that the incorporated
   contents match it. Record the resulting revision and source-to-destination
   correspondence in the originating study.

Permission to explore, prepare a draft, or commit study work does not by itself
approve changing the maintained scientific corpus. Conversely, do not add an
extra approval step when the current task already authorizes the exact action.

## Preserve evidence and limits

Retain each review's input identity, scope, findings, actual checks, unresolved
objections, and independence conditions. A validator can inspect structure and
links; it cannot establish mathematical validity or reviewer independence.

Separate acceptance of a theorem from an accompanying empirical claim whose
reproduction remains unavailable. Express any partial acceptance explicitly;
do not let accepted prose imply the blocked claim was checked.

When corrections change a scientific statement, assumptions, dependencies,
implementation behavior, or empirical evidence, repeat the affected required
reviews. An unchanged prior review is reusable only for the same inputs and
scope, under the adopted policy. Pure formatting repairs need proportionate
checks and must not be used to introduce an unreviewed substantive change.

If later evidence invalidates an accepted claim, mark its current status and
identify downstream dependencies for rechecking. Preserve the historical
evidence while updating the maintained account through the correction process.
